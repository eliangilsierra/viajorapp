# Code of conduct

We want Viajorapp to be a place where people can contribute without being belittled or harassed.

## Our standard

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree about the work, not about the person.
- No harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, or sharing other people's private information.
- Keep feedback specific and actionable. Say what you did not verify.
- Welcome newcomers; explain instead of dismissing.

## Scope

This applies to Issues, Pull Requests, discussions and any space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's report tools on the content, or contact a maintainer through an Issue that does not include sensitive details; maintainers will move the conversation somewhere private.

This text is the project's own short code of conduct. It is not the Contributor Covenant.
